# Website notes (for after the Medium article)

## Box of knowledge visualization
Amirkia's analogy (Medium §8, §10, §12, §22): human knowledge is a box. Most of it is filled
(discovered); some points inside are empty (missed by humans). Deduction and induction, and LLMs, can
fill the empty points inside the box using existing rules and data. A jump (abduction) is thinking
outside the box: it makes the box bigger.

Idea: an interactive box (2D grid or 3D cube):
- filled cells = known knowledge; a few empty holes inside;
- "LLM" button: fills the holes from neighbouring cells (interpolation; stays inside the walls);
- "Jump" button: the wall moves outward and new space appears (e.g. labelled non-Euclidean geometry,
  imaginary numbers, quantum physics, General Relativity);
- Vulcan example: a patch fills a hole inside vs. Einstein expanding the box.
